# Triggers — approved 19 Nov 2026

Source: the work brain's win analysis (17 new-business wins, 70 losses, 20 Nov 2025 – 19 Nov 2026; 12 wins and 39 losses with usable calls). No customer names here by design. Replace this file when the work brain's `company/triggers.md` changes.

## Lead triggers

### T1 — Dated deadline
- **Counts when:** a public, dated operational event is coming up: a route launch, a season start, a new AOC or base opening. The date must be **in the future**.
- **Doesn't count:** dates set by a vendor; dates already passed.
- **Evidence:** 42% of wins vs 3% of losses. The strongest separator.
- **Window:** from the announcement until the date; strongest 4–12 weeks out.
- **Opener angle:** lead with their date. "You're launching [route] on [date]. Who's signing off the risk assessment before then?"

### T2 — Conflict event on their network
- **Counts when:** a **level 1 or 2** event (see below) in the last 2–6 weeks, in a zone where the account has **high** (destination in zone) or **medium** (overflight) exposure.
  - Level 1: airspace closed; new or upgraded ban, advisory or conflict-zone bulletin.
  - Level 2: reported cancellations, diversions or groundings; attack on an airport on their network.
  - Level 3 (fighting reported, no aviation impact yet): **doesn't count** — watch only.
- **Doesn't count:** general concern about a region with no event.
- **Evidence:** 42% of wins vs 23% of losses when the event is specific. Rises to 36% of losses if general concern is counted, so specificity matters.
- **Window:** 2–6 weeks after the event.
- **Opener angle:** their event and their cost. "When [event] hit, what did it cost you on [route]?"

## Supporting triggers (strengthen a lead trigger; never lead on their own)

### T3 — New, resumed or expanding routes
- **Evidence:** 33% of wins vs 15% of losses (25% on discovery calls alone — borderline).
- **Window:** from the announcement to the first flight. Strongest together with T1.
- **Opener angle:** "You're restarting [route] after [suspension]. What's changed in your risk assessment since you last flew it?"

### S1 — Fleet growth or new aircraft
- **Evidence:** 25% of wins vs 8% of losses.
- **Window:** 180 days from the announcement or delivery.

### S2 — New senior hire (security, operations, CEO)
- **Door-opener only.** No evidence it predicts a win. Name the person as the contact to reach; never the reason to reach out.
- **Window:** 90 days from the start date.

Combined: 67% of wins had at least one of T1–T3, vs 33% of losses.

## Not scanned
Insurer or broker raised by the buyer, supplier being replaced, tenders, trials (not public, or neutral). Audit or compliance on its own (doesn't separate).

## Caveats
Small base (one win moves a rate 6–8 points); wins had two calls read and losses one; nothing back-tested; windows and openers are untested.
